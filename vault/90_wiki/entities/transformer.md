# Transformer

## What it is

A neural sequence transduction model introduced in 2017 by researchers at Google Brain and Google Research, relying entirely on attention instead of recurrence or convolution. ([1706.03762](../sources/1706.03762.md))

## Why it matters here

- Set a new state of the art on WMT 2014 English-German translation (28.4 BLEU) at a fraction of earlier training cost. ([1706.03762](../sources/1706.03762.md))
- Generalised to English constituency parsing with little task-specific tuning. ([1706.03762](../sources/1706.03762.md))
