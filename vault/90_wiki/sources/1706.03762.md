# Attention Is All You Need

Vaswani, Shazeer, Parmar, Uszkoreit, Jones, Gomez, Kaiser, Polosukhin (Google Brain / Google Research / University of Toronto), NIPS 2017. Vault file: [1706.03762](../../10_ai/1706.03762.md).

## What it claims

- Proposes the [Transformer](../entities/transformer.md), a sequence transduction model based solely on attention, with no recurrence and no convolution.
- Encoder and decoder are each a stack of N = 6 identical layers; each sub-layer is wrapped in a residual connection followed by layer normalization; d_model = 512 ([Transformer architecture](../topics/transformer-architecture.md)).
- Scaled dot-product attention: softmax(QKᵀ / √d_k) V; the 1/√d_k scaling keeps large dot products from pushing softmax into regions with tiny gradients ([Attention mechanism](../topics/attention-mechanism.md)).
- Multi-head attention runs h = 8 attention functions in parallel on learned projections (d_k = d_v = 64), concatenates and projects them, letting the model attend to different representation subspaces at once ([Multi-head attention](../topics/multi-head-attention.md)).
- Attention is used three ways: encoder self-attention, masked decoder self-attention, and encoder-decoder attention.
- Order information comes from sinusoidal positional encodings added to the embeddings; learned positional embeddings gave nearly identical results.
- A self-attention layer connects all positions with O(1) sequential operations and O(1) maximum path length, versus O(n) for recurrent layers; per-layer cost is O(n²·d).
- Results: 28.4 BLEU on WMT 2014 EN-DE (over 2 BLEU above prior best, including ensembles); 41.8 BLEU on EN-FR after 3.5 days on eight P100 GPUs (abstract; section 6.1 reports 41.0).
- Generalises to English constituency parsing: 91.3 F1 WSJ-only and 92.7 F1 semi-supervised.
- Ablations: single-head attention is 0.9 BLEU worse than the best setting; too many heads also hurts; reducing d_k hurts quality.

## Key terms

self-attention, scaled dot-product attention, multi-head attention, encoder-decoder, positional encoding, residual connection, layer normalization, label smoothing, BLEU
